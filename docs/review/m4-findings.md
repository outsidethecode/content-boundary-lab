## M4-01 in three situations

The register's first wording joined three different events. They are separate because they differ in what is recorded,
what promotion reads and which M0 clause governs them. Only the third is content that is judged hostile after it was
accepted.

| | What happens after acceptance | Where the new fact is recorded | What promotion reads | Test | Governing text |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The vendor's engagement is revoked | `engagements.revoked_at`, one `engagement.revoked` audit event | The delivery's own state, accepted digest and attached verdict; no engagement | `test_revoking_the_vendor_after_acceptance_does_not_stop_a_promotion` | Addendum A9 (a revocation stops un-accepted deliveries from being accepted) and 5.7 (a started operation may finish) |
| 2 | The verdict expires (24 hours, or another engine version is now in use) | Nothing: time passes. `verdicts.valid_until` is now in the past | No clock and no engine; `trust.verdict_binding_problem` asks only for a `no_detection` verdict for H | `test_promotion_asks_only_whether_the_verdict_is_a_no_detection_verdict_for_the_digest` (a unit test of the check, not of a promotion) | Addendum 5.2: validity "is re-checked at acceptance but not at promotion, which is a documented gap" |
| 3 | A newer engine version detects H, when the same bytes are sealed again (a second delivery, in any production) | A `verdicts` row (`detection`, the new engine version, H, the second delivery's id), the second delivery `REJECTED`, one `delivery.rejected` audit event in that delivery's production. Nothing is written against delivery 1 | Only delivery 1's own verdict row, which is the old `no_detection`; not the other `verdicts` rows for H | `test_a_digest_detected_after_acceptance_is_still_promoted_because_promotion_checks_the_accepted_bytes_not_current_evidence` | Addendum 5.2 (the only way out of `ACCEPTED` is to `PROMOTING`); the F-03 limits note in M0.md ("signatures update… rescanning at promotion or a time limit on cached verdicts"), answered in the Addendum by the 24-hour limit (A8) and not by a rescan at promotion |

What the test for situation 3 shows is the behaviour that was accepted, not a control. Delivery 1 reaches `ACCEPTED` for H on
a clean verdict from engine version 1; engine version 2 then records a detection for H on delivery 2; delivery 1 stays
`ACCEPTED`, an internal rescan refuses it (`state_conflict`), and `promote` publishes the accepted bytes, which download as
exactly those bytes. Promotion verifies that the bytes are the accepted bytes (F-04), and does not ask whether any engine has
since judged them differently. Nothing here withdraws an acceptance, consults a threat feed or removes a published asset, and
none is built. M0 never calls an acceptance "final": that reading is AM-25's, from the state diagram.

An operator can look in the `verdicts` table for a `detection` row with a delivery's accepted digest before running `promote`.
The lab does not do it.
